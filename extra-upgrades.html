Extra Upgrades

The power that never drops from a boss: enchants, gems, embellishments, and consumables. These are the upgrades people forget — and the gap between a geared character and a finished one.

The finished-character checklist

  • Enchant every slot — weapon, helm, shoulders, chest, legs, boots, rings.
  • Socket everything — one Eversong Diamond plus Flawless color gems in every socket.
  • Two embellished crafts max — with missives tuned to your best stats.
  • Full consumables — flask, combat potion, oil or stone, food, and an augment rune for raid nights.
  • Spend Mistcrests weekly — and check the Great Vault before upgrading anything.

1 · Enchants

Defaults that fit most specs, and where to look when they don't. Weapon is the most spec-dependent slot — sim it.

Midnight changed the slots. Cloak and bracer enchants were removed; helm and shoulder enchants are new. There is no glove or belt enchant. Legs are handled by Leatherworking armor kits (Strength/Agility) or Tailoring spellthreads (Intellect).
SlotDefault pickEffect & alternatives
Weapon Enchant Weapon - Rite of the Hash'eyNew in 12.1 Chance to gain random secondaries, favoring your highest while above 80% health. Recipe from Jan'sari the Watchful on the Coiled Isle (Renown 5, Zul'jarra's Forces). Alternatives by spec: Berserker's Rage (Haste — e.g. Arms), Jan'alai's Precision (Crit — e.g. Marksmanship), Arcane Mastery (Mastery — e.g. Augmentation), Acuity of the Ren'dorei (Strength — e.g. Brewmaster).
Helm Enchant Helm - Empowered Hex of Leeching Leech, plus healing when defeating enemies outdoors in a Midnight zone. Empowered Rune of Avoidance (Avoidance + speed burst on kills) and Empowered Blessing of Speed (Speed + Vigor charge on kills) are the alternatives; the choice is largely personal preference.
Shoulders Enchant Shoulders - Amirdrassil's Grace or Silvermoon's Mending Tertiary stats: Avoidance (Amirdrassil's Grace, Nature's Grace), Leech (Silvermoon's Mending, Thalassian Recovery), or Speed (Akil'zon's Swiftness, Flight of the Eagle).
Chest Enchant Chest - Mark of the Worldsoul Primary stat — the default for nearly every spec. (Mark of Nalorakk, Mark of the Magister, Mark of the Rootwarden cover niche cases.)
Legs Blood Knight's Armor Kit / Sunfire Silk Spellthread Leatherworking kits for Strength/Agility (Blood Knight's, Forest Hunter's, Thalassian Scout Armor Kit); Tailoring spellthreads for Intellect (Sunfire Silk, Arcanoweave, Bright Linen Spellthread).
Boots Enchant Boots - Lynx's Dexterity Avoidance + Stamina. Alternatives: Shaladrassil's Roots (Leech + Stamina), Farstrider's Hunt (Speed + Stamina).
Rings Enchant Ring - Eyes of the Eagle Critical Strike effectiveness — distinct from plain Crit, and the most common default. Swap to Zul'jin's Mastery, Silvermoon's Alacrity, Silvermoon's Tenacity, or Nature's Fury to match your stat priority.
Exceptions. Death Knights use Runeforging instead of weapon enchants (e.g. Rune of the Fallen Crusader, Rune of Sanguination). Hunters use Engineering scopes instead of weapon enchants (see Consumables).

Standard enchants come from Enchanting; armor kits from Leatherworking; spellthreads from Tailoring. Profession-tool enchants (Amani Perception, Haranir Finesse, etc.) only boost profession stats — they are not combat upgrades.

Flawless cuts are the top tier. One unique Eversong Diamond plus color-matched secondary gems is the standard setup.

Socket your two best secondary stats — the exact cuts are spec-dependent (tanks often mix in Versatile cuts). Heliotrope gems are PvP-only; ignore them for raid and Mythic+.

Eversong Diamonds — unique-equipped (one only)

Indecipherable Eversong Diamond

The most primary stat. The safe default most Season 2 guides recommend.

Powerful Eversong Diamond

Primary stat plus Crit effect per unique gem color — can beat Indecipherable with balanced colors, but it is gear-dependent.

Telluric Eversong Diamond

Primary stat plus mana per unique gem color — popular with mana-hungry casters.

Stoic Eversong Diamond

Primary stat plus movement speed per unique gem color.

Need more sockets? Radiant Jewelbinder adds a socket to a helm, bracers, or belt — PvE gear only. Sold by Vaultkeeper Elysa by the Great Vault in Silvermoon City for 6 Thalassian Token of Merit (the currency you can take from the weekly Great Vault instead of an item).

3 · Embellishments

Crafted gear with built-in or added bonus effects — the biggest "free" power most players leave on the table.

You may equip a maximum of two embellished items at once — a third grants nothing. Embellishments come two ways: gear with a built-in effect, and optional crafting reagents added at craft time. Place crafting work orders with Mar'nah in Silvermoon City.

Slot advice. Bracers are the classic embellishment slot (low stat budget); non-tier cloak, belt, and boots are common too. Avoid embellishments on tier slots (helm/shoulders/chest/gloves/legs) unless a spec guide specifically justifies it — tier pieces are hard to replace. Thalassian Missive of the Quickblade is listed inconsistently across sources — confirm its stats in-game before relying on it.

High-track Season 2 crafts use a Spark of Tides plus Mistcrests for Hero/Myth track (Champion-track crafts use Sparks without Mistcrests). Spend order: a weak weapon or critically low item-level slot first, then your proven embellishment combo, then stat-profile gaps.

Augment rune

Void-Touched Augment Rune — primary stat for 1 hour, the only augment rune in Midnight. It does not persist through death, so use it sparingly unless rich.

Vantus Runes: unconfirmed for Season 2. No Venomous Abyss Vantus Rune was found in any source checked. Season 1 had boss-specific runes; the old rule was Inscription-crafted, +Versatility against one chosen boss, one boss per weekly reset. Check the Inscription trainer and the Auction House in-game before assuming they exist.

Raid convenience: Cauldron of Sin'dorei Flasks and Voidlight Potion Cauldron let the party take the flask or potion of their choice.

5 · Other easy-to-miss upgrades

Upgrade tracks, Vault thresholds, tier acquisition, and the drops worth prioritizing.

Mistcrests — the Season 2 upgrade system

Five track-locked tiers — Adventurer, Veteran, Champion, Hero, Myth — each only upgrades its own track, at 20 crests per upgrade level plus a small gold fee. There are no Valorstones in Season 2.

  • Weekly cap is about 100 per tier per character on a cumulative catch-up curve (the cap rises roughly 100/week); over-cap rewards downgrade a tier.
  • Upgrade vendor: Cuzolth in Silvermoon City's Bazaar. Higher crests exchange 1:1 down via Vaskarn without counting against the cap.
  • Warband alts pay 50% fewer crests — spread gearing across alts where sensible.
  • Myth Mistcrests come from Mythic Venomous Abyss and Mythic+ +9 and above (+2/+3 give Champion, +4+ give Hero).

Great Vault thresholds

Raid slots unlock at 2, 4, and 6 eligible Venomous Abyss boss kills; Mythic+ slots unlock at 1, 4, and 8 dungeons. Check the Vault before spending crests on anything it might replace.

Tier acquisition

Tier tokens drop from five bosses: Entombed Sentinels (Hands), The Lost Explorers (Shoulders), Vashnik the Malignant (Chest), Sszorak (Legs), The Twin Fangs (Helm) — plus the flexible Slumbering Coil Curio from Ula'tek. See every Season 2 drop in the loot browser, and the raid boss guides, before you spend. A Revival Catalyst exists in Silvermoon City for converting eligible gear into tier pieces; its exact Season 2 charge cadence is unconfirmed, so check in-game.

Prioritize the last two bosses. The Coiled Altar and Ula'tek drop item level 344 gear — roughly 10 item levels above the normal Myth-track cap.

Remember: Thalassian Token of Merit can be taken from the weekly Great Vault instead of an item — six of them buy a Radiant Jewelbinder socket.
