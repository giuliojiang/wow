Midnight Season 2 · Patch 12.1← Morrigan's Tips

Spend Voidcores where upgrades overlap.

A focused bonus-roll route for Demon Hunters: use dungeon rolls on deep loot tables, then reserve extras for raid pieces that have no substitute.

New: browse every drop from each Season 2 dungeon and raid boss in the Loot tables tab.

Before you roll Voidcore rules ↗

  • A Nebulous Voidcore rolls one dungeon pool or one raid boss.
  • The dungeon priorities below assume a Mythic+ 10 roll.
  • Looted items leave that difficulty's roll pool, so repeat rolls become more targeted.

Devourer priority path

Start with Murder Row for the densest useful pool. Shift to Voidscar Arena and Den of Nalorakk for catalyst slots, then chase Ula'tek's irreplaceable raid pieces.

Havoc priority path
